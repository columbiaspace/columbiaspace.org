As commercial spaceflight expands and NASA's Artemis program prepares to return humans to the Moon, understanding the short- and long-term health risks astronauts face has become increasingly urgent. Our work spans plant biology, microbiology, human physiology, and hardware development, with findings that aim to protect astronaut health while contributing to medical science and technology on Earth.

Because space biology sits at the intersection of several disciplines, we train members from the ground up.
